# MyGreatestStrength
This is a collaborative project from the students of Bachelor's of Arts Semester 6 2022 + A client from TH Köln University

“My Greatest Strength” is a single player, 2D, PC game for children suffering from anxiety and/or depression to reinforce a positive self-image.

Concept:
  Player takes on the role of a lemonade stand owner and they are confronted with several situations that occur around the stand and its customers. For each predicament, there are two   options. By choosing which lemonade tap to prepare the orders from—right or left—players can make their selections. There are a total of 42 events. After each game part, the player's   choices are logged.
  At the end of the game, their scores are calculated based on their answers to determine what their strongest traits are. These attributes are shown to the players along with           instances of how they might be useful in real life.
